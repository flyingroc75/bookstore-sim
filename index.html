📚 Opening Day

You have 500 coins and a stocked shop: 6 non-fiction (green) and 6 fiction (purple) on the shelves.

Buyers go to their genre's shelf, take a book, then queue at the sell counter (right) — walk there to ring them up. Each book sells at its own price, fixed but hidden from you: at the buy counter you only ever see a range.

Sellers queue at the buy counter (left) — haggle with them there. Books you buy go in your arms — carry them to the matching shelf to stock them.

Everyone has a patience meter. Too slow, or the shelf is empty, and they walk out. The day lasts 4 minutes.

Walk by clicking / tapping where you want to go, and use the big action button when it appears.

Every book has its own hidden sell price — you only ever see an appraisal range. Sellers may open above the true value. Profit is made or lost at the buy counter.
